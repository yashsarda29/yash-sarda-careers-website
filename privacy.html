Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Who we are

NextMove Careers offers one-on-one career guidance by Yash Sarda. This policy explains what we collect on this website and how we use it.

What we collect

Before you start a Career Check test or download a report or guide, we ask for:

  • your name,
  • your email address, and
  • your mobile number.

If you choose Continue with Google, Google shares only your name and email address with us. We do not get your Google password or access to anything else in your Google account.

We also record which test or download you asked for, the page you were on, and the date and time.

Your test answers are not collected. They stay in your own browser and are never sent to us.

Why we collect it

  • To follow up with you about career guidance, by email, phone or WhatsApp.
  • To understand which tests and guides are useful, so we can improve them.

Where it is stored

Your details are saved in a private Google Sheet that only NextMove Careers can access. Your browser also remembers your details on your own device, so you are not asked again; you can clear this by clearing your browser's site data.

Sharing

We never sell, rent or share your details with anyone else.

How long we keep it

We keep your details while they are useful for guiding you, and delete them when you ask us to.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your details at any time. Message us on WhatsApp at +91 88506 28288 and we will do it within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
