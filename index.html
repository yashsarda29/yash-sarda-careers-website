    Questions

    Before you message.

    01Is the first call really free?

    Yes. The first 15 minutes are free. We use them to understand where you are and which session will help most.

    02How much do sessions cost?

    Sessions start at ₹99 for 30 minutes, and any 8 different sessions cost ₹799 as a bundle. The first 15-minute call is free. You can pay by UPI or any other mode.

    03How are sessions held?

    Every session is a 30-minute one-on-one video call, in Hindi or English. A phone or laptop and a stable connection is all you need. Afterwards you get a written summary and 7 days of WhatsApp follow-up.

    04What if I need to reschedule or cancel?

    You can reschedule for free up to 12 hours before your session. If you cancel 24 hours or more before, you get a full refund.

    05Can I mix or book several sessions?

    Yes. Start anywhere: a CV review today, a mock interview next week. Practice-heavy topics like interviews, GDs and cases work best over a few sessions, so the ₹799 bundle of 8 is usually the better deal.

    06I'm still in school. Can you help?

    Yes. I help with stream choice after 10th and course choice after 12th.
